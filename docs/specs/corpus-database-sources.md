# Corpus database sources

## Vocabulary rules

Add `SourceWindowState`, `DiscoveryPageState`, `DiscoveryClaimState`, `ArtifactRequestState`, `ArtifactRole`, and `RetentionOwnerKind` as typed strings with `Valid()` in `internal/corpus/model`, following [AcceptanceState](../../internal/corpus/model/intake.go). Preserve frozen contracts: [ArtifactInventoryEntry.Role](../../internal/corpus/model/observations.go) stays a string; the adapter checks membership before persistence. SQL CHECK sets must match the constants. Change constants, constraints, and the object inventory together under [chain mutability](corpus-database.md#chain-mutability-and-verification). Hosted M1.3 schema checks must cover every member, unknown values, and state-dependent nullability; adapter checks must cover transitions and stale fences.

Reuse [source/api.DiscoveryMode](../../internal/corpus/source/api/ports.go) for window mode and [model.RequestPurpose](../../internal/corpus/model/observations.go) for purpose. Do not move or duplicate their constants. Tables, identities, indexes, and immutable receipts remain owned by the [intake ledger](corpus-database.md#intake-ledger-and-revision-acceptance) and [access paths](corpus-database.md#persistence-dependencies-and-access-paths).

**Decision:** Keep one checked, non-null state per accounting row and the value sets below. Make `incomplete` resumable without resetting its window. Preserve failed-page evidence and retain blobs until every dependency has protection.
**Why:** The [workflow contract](corpus-workflows.md#source-intake-and-child-lifetime) resumes incomplete windows and requires launch accounting. Terminal `incomplete`, blanket page failure, and unconditional edge removal would defeat recovery or erase evidence. [Writer 00002](../../deploy/corpusmigrations/writer/00002_admission_and_ledger.sql) binds retry time to the count `pending_retry > 0`, not to an outcome name; request eligibility below is a separate proposed constraint.
**Rejected:** Timestamp-derived states, PostgreSQL enums, lookup tables, reopening terminal evidence, and resetting budgets on recovery.
**Detail:** Text CHECKs avoid separate enum types or vocabulary rows, but require synchronized SQL and Go sets. Resumption cannot repair a spent fetch budget. Revision blobs remain retained until an expiry policy exists. Only listed transitions are allowed. Terminal rows permit identical receipt replay, never mutation. SQL checks enforce row shape; fenced adapters enforce transitions, cross-row conditions, and [atomic ledger acceptance](corpus-database.md#intake-ledger-and-revision-acceptance). The [workflow contracts](corpus-workflows.md#workflow-contracts) name all six applies used here: `ApplyDiscoveryPage`, `ApplyCapture`, `ApplyConversion`, `ApplyEvidence`, `ApplySnapshot`, and `SealRelease`. Receipt, claim, and recovery substeps below are proposed adapter operations under those owners, not new workflow activity names.

## Source window state

`ingest.source_window.state`. `SourceSync` owns it under the `source_window` lease selected by [IntakeSubject](../../internal/corpus/run/scopes.go). Freeze the window inputs once. Hold the committed cursor at its initial value until `complete`. `complete` and `failed` are terminal. A replacement window gets a new key and preserves its predecessor's accounting.

| Value | Meaning | Enters from | Transition owner and condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `frozen` | No page is accepted; discovery can proceed. | Insert; `incomplete` | `SourceSync` creates the window, or resumes it after its blocker clears with no accepted page. |
| `progressing` | At least one page is accepted; discovery or launch accounting can proceed. No page has an intrinsic failure. | `frozen`, `incomplete` | `ApplyDiscoveryPage` accepts a page, or recovery resumes remaining work. Stay here while more accounting is possible. |
| `complete` | Every planned page is accepted, the final page proves exhaustion, no discovery continuation or unresolved coverage finding remains, and required child launches are acknowledged. | `frozen`, `progressing`, `incomplete` | `ApplyDiscoveryPage` acceptance or reconciliation satisfies all conditions and commits the final `NextCursor`. |
| `incomplete` | Coverage or launch accounting is unresolved and cannot currently proceed. No page has an intrinsic failure. Keep a non-empty reason. | `frozen`, `progressing` | `ApplyDiscoveryPage` records a coverage blocker, exhausted budget, deadline, or unresolved launch accounting. |
| `failed` | A page has an intrinsic terminal failure. Keep its reason. | `frozen`, `progressing`, `incomplete` | `ApplyDiscoveryPage` records the page failure and window failure atomically. |

Apply intrinsic page failure before incompleteness, and completion before ordinary progress. Exhausting a request's source-retry allowance while the window remains eligible fails that page. Exhausting the window's aggregate budget or deadline makes the window incomplete and preserves nonterminal page states. Window expiry alone is not an intrinsic page failure. Require a non-empty reason exactly for `incomplete` and `failed`; clear it on resumption and retain the earlier outcome receipt. On window failure, mark only nonterminal pages `failed` with `window_closed`; preserve accepted pages and existing failure reasons. Incompleteness never fabricates a page failure. Resume the same window only within its frozen allowances; a spent request budget or deadline forbids further fetches. Receipt and launch reconciliation may continue without new fetches. Cancellation permits acknowledging already-started children; [workflow cancellation](corpus-workflows.md#source-intake-and-child-lifetime) stops new launches. Lease loss or cancellation blocks the old owner; a newly admitted owner reconciles under a fresh fence and cannot erase cancellation or reset budgets. [Closure](corpus-write-path.md#closure-accounting-and-evidence) owns late document acceptance and snapshot coverage. Open question 1 covers source-window gap projection.

## Discovery page state

`ingest.discovery_page.state`. Purpose is `listing` or `number`. `SourceSync` records request planning and staged receipts under its window lease; `ApplyDiscoveryPage` alone accepts page evidence. These intermediate receipts never commit observations, launch intents, or cursor progress separately. Page eligibility uses the lease's `next_eligible_at` and shared host pacing; it adds no independent retry clock. A fetch holds its host slot through a [discovery claim](#discovery-claims-and-lock-order).

| Value | Meaning | Enters from | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `requested` | A frozen request plan awaits a successful capture. | Insert; `requested` | Record the plan. A retryable source outcome retains this state only while source allowance remains. |
| `captured` | Staged bytes and an immutable capture receipt exist; no decoded result exists. | `requested` | Fenced Fetch receipt commit. The decoder must verify mounted bytes before reading them. |
| `decoded` | The sandbox result passed `DecodedDiscoveryPage.Validate`; its digest, cursor proposal, completeness flag, and findings are recorded. No page evidence is accepted yet. | `captured` | Fenced decode receipt commit. |
| `accepted` | Terminal. Observations, findings, launch intents, and the cursor proposal are committed with the acceptance result. | `decoded` | `ApplyDiscoveryPage`; window completion has the additional conditions above. |
| `failed` | Terminal. The request's source-retry allowance ended while the window remained eligible, infrastructure retries ended (`infrastructure_retry_exhausted`), policy rejected the request, capture integrity or decoding failed, or another page's intrinsic failure closed the window. Keep the precise reason. | `requested`, `captured`, `decoded` | `ApplyDiscoveryPage` records the outcome; `window_closed` means closure by another page's failure. |

CHECKs require no capture/decode receipt in `requested`, a complete capture receipt in `captured`, and both receipt groups in `decoded` or `accepted`. Decode fields, including nullable `complete`, are present together; absence is not `false`. Only `accepted` has an acceptance result reference. `failed` retains any earlier receipt groups without claiming acceptance. Require a reason exactly for `failed`. Never overwrite a recorded receipt on retry.

[CursorPosition.Validate](../../internal/corpus/model/observations.go) requires an incomplete proposal to have `NextCursor == CommittedCursor`. It does not prove exhaustion or bind cursors to database state. Persist the request cursor and the ledger-assigned page ordinal separately from the window checkpoint. Validate decoder cursor fields against the frozen `DiscoverRequest` and preserve the raw receipt. The decoder's `Cursor.Ordinal` is not the ledger page ordinal. Only complete-window accounting commits the final `NextCursor` to the window. `ApplyDiscoveryPage` must validate window and slice identity, the ledger-assigned page ordinal, and complete coverage across all pages. Pagination continuations are request plans, not committed progress. Accepting `complete = false` preserves findings and observations; it leaves the window progressing or incomplete. A decode rejection follows retry Q and cannot recapture unchanged input.

## Artifact request state

`ingest.artifact_request.state`. Purpose is `detail` or `artifact`. `DocumentIngest` owns requests under its `document_version` lease. It plans detail requests from listing provenance; `ApplyCapture` records artifact requests from decoded inventory. Use `owner_kind = observation` and the opaque parent observation key; for artifact requests this is [source/api.ArtifactRequest.ParentObservation](../../internal/corpus/source/api/ports.go). Freeze the request identity, purpose, required flag, owner, and plan. Recovery reuses rows and source-attempt accounting; a new processing attempt may create linked new work but cannot bypass source eligibility.

Require `expected_role` exactly when purpose is `artifact`, using `ArtifactRole`; otherwise use NULL. Preserve `expected_response_kind` as non-null text: detail allows the empty value and the other values admitted by [validateResponseKind](../../internal/corpus/source/api/ports.go); artifact requires the empty value. The empty value supports sources with one detail response. Follow-up origin checks remain owned by [FollowUpRequest.ValidateOrigin](../../internal/corpus/model/observations.go) and [RequestPlan.ValidateOrigin](../../internal/corpus/source/api/ports.go).

| Value | Meaning | Enters from | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `due` | Waiting for eligibility; no successful Fetch receipt. | Insert; `leased` | Creation, retryable source outcome with allowance left, or recovery after lease loss. Only a current owner may return a row to due. |
| `leased` | The current document lease holder has claimed the request and its host slot. | `due` | Claim after both request and host eligibility, within the frozen allowances. |
| `fetched` | Staged bytes and immutable transport receipt exist; Bronze registration is pending. | `leased` | Fenced Fetch receipt commit. Release the host slot once. |
| `verified` | Terminal capture success. Staged length and hash match the receipt; purpose-appropriate capture references and retention are registered. | `fetched` | `ApplyCapture`. Artifact requests also register the artifact and observation link. Detail capture does not invent an artifact role or imply successful decoding. |
| `failed` | Terminal policy, permanent source, size/budget, or integrity failure. Keep its reason. | `due`, `leased`, `fetched` | Fenced `ApplyCapture` outcome; Q forbids retrying unchanged bad input. |
| `exhausted` | Terminal. The source retry allowance ended without a successful Fetch receipt. | `due`, `leased` | Fenced outcome or eligibility reconciliation. |
| `expired` | Terminal. The owning processing attempt closed or its deadline passed before registration. | `due`, `leased`, `fetched` | Fenced owner closure or recovery reconciliation; never the stale worker. |

CHECK `(state = 'due') = (next_eligible_at IS NOT NULL)`. Require a complete receipt in `fetched` and `verified`, none in `due`, `leased`, or `exhausted`; `failed` and `expired` preserve any existing receipt. Require a non-empty reason exactly for `failed`, `exhausted`, and `expired`. A verified receipt never regresses on later decode or quality failure. Reconcile committed outcomes before expiry; otherwise owner closure takes precedence over retry exhaustion. Release or reconcile host slots exactly once on every exit from `leased`. Infrastructure recovery reuses a fetched receipt instead of downloading again.

Both request tables use [retry classes I/S/Q/X](corpus-write-path.md#stage-contract), not new retry policy. Count each durable source attempt once, including a successful fetch; worker loss, transport replay of a commit, and cancellation alone add no source attempt. Request source exhaustion uses the request's frozen attempt and time allowances, not an unconditional fifth failure or the window's aggregate budget or deadline. Replanning never resets them. Provider batch reconciliation X does not authorize recapturing these requests.

## Discovery claims and lock order

A discovery claim authorizes one fetch for one `requested` page and holds one host slot (`in_flight`). The frozen `request_plan` URL authority selects the host. A claim belongs to the `source_window` lease; it is neither a page state nor a workflow activity. Artifact requests use `leased` for their slot and follow the same lock order.

**Decision:** Identify a claim by country, window, page, and claim ordinal, stored on the page. Authorize transitions through the current lease, not the execution that issued the claim.
**Why:** Recovery may run under a different scheduled workflow ID. The [workflow contract](corpus-workflows.md#source-intake-and-child-lifetime) requires recovery across executions; the lease and admission epoch establish current ownership.
**Rejected:** Workflow IDs, result keys, or delivery recipients as claim identity; receipt scans as slot state; a separate claim table when a page can hold only one slot.
**Detail:** Add the two missing columns below to [writer `00003`](../../deploy/corpusmigrations/writer/00003_sources.sql) under [chain mutability](corpus-database.md#chain-mutability-and-verification). Add both to the `mutable_columns` list in the discovery-page branch of `ingest.check_source_request_change()`, used by `discovery_page_frozen`, and the `GRANT UPDATE (...) ON ingest.discovery_page TO banhmi_corpus_writer_runtime`. Reject decreasing `claim_ordinal` in that trigger. Add `DiscoveryClaimState` under the [vocabulary rules](#vocabulary-rules). Keep the existing terminal-row and immutable-receipt guards.

| Column | Proposed definition |
|---|---|
| `claim_ordinal` | `integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (claim_ordinal >= 0)` |
| `claim_state` | Nullable `text CHECK (claim_state IN ('issued', 'settled', 'released'))` |

Add `CHECK ((claim_ordinal = 0) = (claim_state IS NULL))` and `CHECK (state = 'requested' OR claim_state IS DISTINCT FROM 'issued')`. These permit an unclaimed failed page, a released failed page, and settled captured/decoded/accepted pages. They preserve the existing receipt-nullability CHECKs; a capture must settle its claim in the same UPDATE. Fenced adapters enforce the transitions below.

The claim attempt key is `request-` plus 32 hex digits derived from window key, page key, and claim ordinal. Every claim receipt carries it. Each settlement records it in `last_attempt_key`. `claim_ordinal` counts issues; `source_attempts` counts recorded source outcomes. Existing `infrastructure_attempts` starts at 1 and ranges through 3; it reserves the initial infrastructure attempt and advances only for a replacement of a released claim. It is not a count of losses. `retry_after` and `last_attempt_key` already exist on the page. `next_allowed_at`, `in_flight`, `max_in_flight`, and `min_interval` already exist on `ingest.source_rate_limit`; no other columns are added.

Each row below commits its state, accounting, and receipt atomically. Claim receipts bind claim identity and operation; window receipts use immutable operation keys, and resumption refers to its closure receipt. Replays change no counter or slot. Reconcile committed outcomes before release, replacement, or closure. Source and infrastructure retry policy remains owned by [retry classes I/S/Q/X](corpus-write-path.md#stage-contract).

| Operation or exit | Transition | Source attempt | Infrastructure ordinal | Host slot | Durable marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Issue | NULL, `settled`, or `released` to `issued`; require all eligibility checks and reject `released` at 3 | 0 | +1 only from `released` below 3; otherwise unchanged | Acquire once; set `next_allowed_at = now + min_interval` after checking host eligibility | Advance `claim_ordinal`; issue receipt |
| Successful capture | `issued` to `settled`; page `captured` | +1 | Unchanged | Release once | `settled`; capture receipt; `last_attempt_key` |
| Retryable source failure | `issued` to `settled`; apply page/window allowance rules | +1 | Unchanged | Release once; advance host pacing to at least `Retry-After` | `settled`; outcome receipt; `retry_after`; `last_attempt_key` |
| Intrinsic failure with a source outcome | `issued` to `settled`; page and window `failed` | +1 | Unchanged | Release once | `settled`; failure receipt and reason; `last_attempt_key` |
| Policy rejection before fetch | Page and window `failed`; an issued claim becomes `released` | 0 | Unchanged | Release once only if issued | Failure receipt and reason; `released` if claimed |
| Infrastructure loss | `issued` to `released`; at ordinal 3 fail page and window with `infrastructure_retry_exhausted` | 0 | Unchanged | Release once | `released`; loss receipt; exhaustion reason at 3 |
| Window closes `incomplete` | Every `issued` claim becomes `released`; preserve page states | 0 | Unchanged | Release each issued slot once | `released`; closure receipt; window reason |
| Window closes `failed` | Every `issued` claim becomes `released`; apply page closure rules | 0 | Unchanged | Release each issued slot once | `released`; closure receipt; page reasons |
| Late success for latest released claim | `released` to `settled`; page `captured` | +1 | Unchanged | No release | `settled`; capture receipt; `last_attempt_key` |
| Late retryable failure for latest released claim | `released` to `settled`; apply page/window allowance rules | +1 | Unchanged | No release; still honor host `Retry-After` | `settled`; outcome receipt; `retry_after`; `last_attempt_key` |
| Late intrinsic source failure for latest released claim | `released` to `settled`; page and window `failed` | +1 | Unchanged | No release | `settled`; failure receipt and reason; `last_attempt_key` |
| Resumption or replacement denied at 3 | Latest claim is `released`; fail page and window with `infrastructure_retry_exhausted` | 0 | Unchanged | No release | Exhaustion receipt and reason |
| Eligible resumption | No claim transition; apply window resumption rules | 0 | Unchanged | No release | Resumption receipt |
| Decode or integrity failure after capture | Page and window `failed`; retain the settled claim and earlier receipts | 0 | Unchanged | No release | Failure receipt and reason |
| Stale outcome or identical replay | No transition; reject stale outcome or return committed receipt | 0 | Unchanged | No release | Existing claim/terminal state or committed receipt |

Infrastructure accounting: releasing a claim records the loss or closure immediately, even if the window never resumes. Only issuing its replacement advances the infrastructure ordinal. Thus an unreplaced loss is absent from that ordinal, not from durable accounting. [Closure accounting](corpus-write-path.md#closure-accounting-and-evidence) uses unique item outcomes and explicit gaps, not attempt counters. A released claim at ordinal 3 cannot authorize a fourth infrastructure attempt. A settled source failure follows S eligibility; it is not an infrastructure replacement. Resumption resets no counter or allowance.

Late outcomes: first look up a committed receipt, even after a terminal page or a later claim. Return identical replays and reject changed payloads under the [replay rule](corpus-write-path.md#closure-accounting-and-evidence). Otherwise only the current owner may settle the latest `released` claim while its page remains `requested`. Success preserves the window state and reason; failures follow the page/window precedence rules. Before replacement or closure, reconcile available capture receipts so committed evidence cannot be mistaken for an abandoned fetch. Uncommitted bytes from stale outcomes remain staging objects under that same write-path contract. They cannot reopen terminal pages or advance coverage. Preserve all earlier committed receipt groups when closing a page.

Ownership: validate admission (enabled, admitted writer, matching epoch), the owning lease (matching owner, fence and epoch, unexpired), and the claim or intent in one transaction. Issuer workflow IDs, Run IDs, and attempt keys are provenance, not the authority for a later owner. A reconciliation-mode `SourceSync` may reconcile under the window lease but may not issue claims. Cancellation allows release, closure, and acknowledgement of already-started children; it does not authorize fresh capture or evidence acceptance. A fresh admitted owner reconciles retained receipts without erasing cancellation. Follow the [workflow cancellation rules](corpus-workflows.md#source-intake-and-child-lifetime) and [write-path acceptance gate](corpus-write-path.md#closure-accounting-and-evidence).

**Decision:** Keep the original launch delivery identity unchanged. Record transfer to country control as an immutable `ingest.execution_result` receipt under the window lease.
**Why:** The delivery identifies the child to start. Transfer changes reconciliation authority. A stable control resource lets separately scheduled control executions consume the same intent.
**Rejected:** Rewriting `recipient_workflow_id`, binding transfer to one scheduled execution, or adding generic recipient-resource columns for this source-specific handoff.
**Detail:** Use `substep = source-launch-transfer` and the delivery deduplication key as `shard`. Bind the receipt to country, window key, original delivery identity, receipt reference, expected epoch and hash, and target `(resource_kind = control, resource_key = country)`. `corpus-control/<country>` names that resource; it does not replace the delivery recipient. Under the window lock, allow one transfer per intent. Identical repeats return its receipt; changed bindings fail. Transfer removes the window owner's authority to consume that intent without setting `acknowledged_at`. Only `ControlReconcile` may consume it under current admission and its current control lease. Historical workflow IDs do not authorize consumption. Acknowledge the original delivery only after confirming the unchanged child's start under the [workflow contract](corpus-workflows.md#source-intake-and-child-lifetime). Transfer does not override cancellation.

**Decision:** Acquire locks in this order: country admission `FOR SHARE`; owning lease `FOR UPDATE`; source window `FOR UPDATE`; discovery pages by `page_key`; host rows by `host_key`; artifact requests by `request_key`; blobs by `sha256`. Use `FOR UPDATE` for those page, host, request, and blob locks. Sort keys in byte order. Acquire all needed existing locks before inserts or updates that could take foreign-key locks. If another earlier row becomes necessary, abort and restart. Insert multiple new rows in fixed table and UNIQUE-key order. For Bronze use `source_record`, `blob`, `observation_revision`, `artifact`, `observation_artifact`, then `blob_retention_reference`; this respects their foreign-key dependencies.
**Why:** [PostgreSQL 17 Table 13.3](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html#LOCKING-ROWS) shows that `KEY SHARE` conflicts with `UPDATE`, but not `NO KEY UPDATE` or `SHARE`. Conflicts alone are not deadlocks: a consistent acquisition order prevents cycles. Admission `SHARE` blocks epoch rotation without blocking other admitted writers. In `00002` and `00003`, no foreign key references `country_admission` or `execution_lease`. `discovery_page` and `artifact_request` reference `source_window`; only `artifact_request` references `source_rate_limit`. Their inserts take `KEY SHARE` on those parents. Prelocking each parent prevents a later foreign-key check from reversing the order.
**Rejected:** Per-operation lock orders; weakening lease locks without a foreign-key conflict to solve; changing host modes while retaining request-before-host acquisition; table or advisory locks that serialize unrelated resources.
**Detail:** Keep the admission and materialized lease lock modes in [ledger.sql](../../internal/corpus/store/intake/queries/ledger.sql) and [sources.sql](../../internal/corpus/store/intake/queries/sources.sql); `ledger.sql` needs no lock-mode change. Keep existing window, request, host, and blob modes too. The consuming slice must change `sources.sql` ordering: claim, outcome, recovery, and expiry currently lock/update a request before its host; acceptance updates the page before the window. Prelock window, pages, hosts, requests, and blobs as applicable before these queries and before request or Bronze inserts. Lock all relevant hosts for closure in byte order. Prelock blobs before any referencing insert, including before `AddBlobRetentionReference`; taking that lock only after another insert's `KEY SHARE` risks an upgrade deadlock. Discover candidate identities without locks, acquire locks in order, then recheck identity, eligibility, and lease expiry after waiting. Preserve materialized-lock validation and fresh-snapshot budget checks.

Hosted M1.3 checks must cover every table row and replay, late outcomes after incomplete closure, a released claim never replaced, three losses across recovery and closure, transfer consumption by a second scheduled control execution, and preservation of committed capture receipts. Race closure against request registration and request claim/settlement on shared hosts in opposite input order. Include concurrent blob registration and retention insertion. These are required checks, not evidence already obtained.

## Artifact role

**Decision:** Keep `ArtifactRole = main | appendix | original_scan` for both Bronze role columns and artifact request expectations. Validate every observation link against its artifact's role.
**Why:** These are exactly the combined emitted values in the four decoders, not a common filename grammar: [VBPL](../../internal/country/vn/sources/vbpl/decoder.go), [Công Báo](../../internal/country/vn/sources/congbao/decoder.go), [Vanban](../../internal/country/vn/sources/vanban/decoder.go), and [SBV Hanoi](../../internal/country/vn/sources/sbvhanoi/decoder.go). Only VBPL relabels main PDFs as `original_scan`. The [parts evidence](document-parts.md#current-state-and-evidence) describes legacy OCR selection, not a new role-based OCR restriction.
**Rejected:** Unchecked database roles, a lookup table, and speculative `attachment` or `version_snapshot` values.
**Detail:** `main` means source-presented document text; `appendix` means a labelled supplement; `original_scan` means a declared main PDF rendition, not proof of scanned content. Format validity remains in [ArtifactFormatValid](../../internal/corpus/model/observations.go). Detection and gates determine usability and authority. Scanned attachments follow the [part gate](document-parts.md#part-model-and-deterministic-parsing), including when the body has binding text. Add a role only with its producer, consumers, constants, and CHECK values. Conversion-output roles belong to writer file `00005`, not this set.

`bronze.blob_retention_reference.owner_kind`. Edges protect dependencies, not workflow success. `00003` admits the five kinds below; insert build and release edges only when their consumers exist. **Decision:** Keep the five owner kinds and make edge removal depend on protected closure, not execution termination or release tombstones alone.
**Why:** The database's [provenance contract](corpus-database.md#conversion-provenance-and-decisions) retains reports, maps, comparisons, recipes, and decisions; [release retention](corpus-database-releases.md#releases-delivery-and-retention) also protects archives, pins, and holds. Terminal work can still supply that evidence. A document revision edge needs the full revision key because a document identity alone does not select an immutable closure.
**Rejected:** Deleting every execution edge at its terminal result, or every build edge on failure, without a dependency check.
**Detail:** Edge insertion, transfer, removal, and garbage collection serialize on the blob so a new reference cannot race deletion. Follow the parent spec's immutable blob/artifact rules. Absence of edges permits consideration of object deletion, never deletion of immutable catalog rows. Staging objects remain governed by the [write-path staging contract](corpus-write-path.md#closure-accounting-and-evidence).

## Open questions

1. How should standalone source-window gaps enter the public projection? [00002](../../deploy/corpusmigrations/writer/00002_admission_and_ledger.sql) requires `evidence_gap.acceptance_id`, but [derived coverage](corpus-database.md#migration-chains-and-generated-stores) requires unresolved window gaps with a stable source `gap_key`. Reading window states alone does not settle that identity or historical watermark. M1.3 must preserve coverage receipts; agree the gap representation before implementing its projection. Do not fabricate revision acceptance or silently prescribe a nullable migration.
2. When may observation and document revision retention edges expire? The [retention authority](corpus-database-releases.md#releases-delivery-and-retention) sets release retention, not Bronze or revision expiry. Keep these edges until a reviewed policy protects every remaining dependency; immutable rows do not by themselves require permanent blob storage.
