What your agent gets

{{.Name}} does not answer questions. It returns database evidence — text extracted verbatim from official government sources — and your agent decides the answer:

  • Exact citations at the provision level: {{.Citation}}, with the document number.
  • A validity badge on every hit — repealed, superseded or not-yet-effective law is never presented as current.
  • The official source_url for every document, so anyone can verify the wording at the origin.
  • Confirmed amendment/repeal relations and verbatim amending clauses.
  • Explicit coverage gaps (corpus_status, quality_gaps) instead of confident guesses.
